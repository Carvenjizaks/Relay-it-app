## Format (Token-Efficient)

**Memory Cascade:**
- **3 days back:** Quarter summary (25% detail) - just the headline
- **2 days back:** Half summary (50% detail) - key outcomes only
- **Yesterday:** Half summary (50% detail) - building on previous

**What to Report:**
1. What's **outstanding** (still open)
2. What **still needs to be done** (next actions)
3. What's **urgent** (blockers or deadlines)

**What NOT to Include:**
- Detailed task breakdowns
- Step-by-step explanations
- Full transcripts or logs
